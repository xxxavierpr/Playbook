The line was sloping down, and the histogram had only just gone negative (about −0.02 at 08:54, about −0.09 at 08:55). That is a late tick far from zero, not a fresh cross near zero with the HA bar.

## Better entry — what to wait for

Same button Monday: solid red 1-minute HA cross down through EMA20, one contract, about $0.15, spread at or under $0.02.

Take that cross when the bar also has all three:

- Underlying **close** under session VWAP. A wick that closes back above VWAP is not confirmation.
- RSI(14) still at or above 30, ideally falling through 50 with the red HA. Under 30, skip.
- MACD histogram negative and the MACD line near zero. A line still up around +0.3 after a green run is the 08:55 probe. Let it go and keep the ticket.

On this tape that set did not show up at the open. It also did not show up as a new cross when price finally closed under VWAP at 09:54 CT (751.76 vs VWAP 751.93).
