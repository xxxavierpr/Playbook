## Was the bounce entry good?

No. It was legal under the override and cleanly executed, and it bought a stall.

### What was right

- The bounce rule passed as written. It was a first touch (the 08:59 low was 0.39 over EMA20), on a green HA bar, closing 0.67 over EMA20 and 1.78 over the ticket's VWAP, with RSI14 under 70. The first-hour EMA check passed on the shipped seed (HA close 743.575 against 743.08) and on the prior-session seed (743.34).
- Execution matched the book. The fill came at the ask 2 seconds after the bar closed, and the stop was half the fill, resting at the broker. The 09:06:50 API timeout cost nothing.
- The stop was right. QQQ has not traded above 744.20 since 09:01. It fell to 739.83 by 10:38 and was 743.26 at 11:49.

### What was wrong

1. **Nothing dipped.** From 08:43 to 08:59 QQQ sat in a 742.58–743.97 box, while EMA20 climbed 1.78, from 741.29 at 08:42 to 743.08 at 09:00. The 09:00 low, 743.14, sat inside the five lows before it (743.085–743.39). The touch was the average catching up with a flat price, not a pullback to a rising one. EMA20's climb had already slowed from 0.12 a bar (08:45–08:50) to 0.06 (08:55–09:00).
2. **Momentum had already turned.** The MACD line peaked at 08:49 and crossed under its signal line at 08:52. At the entry the histogram was −0.063 and the line was a third off its peak (0.58 against 0.86). The 08:53 high was only 0.35 over the 08:42 high. Xavier's 09:16 chart shows the same thing: MACD red, price coiling on EMA20.
3. **The target was outside the box and the stop inside it.** The entry was in the top 16% of the box. The +20% price was over the box top: 744.1 on the ticket, nearer 744.2 on the marks. The 09:01 bar did break out, to 744.20, which is still the session high at 11:49, and the 09:02 check read +10.7%. The −50% stop filled with QQQ near 742.9, still inside the box. 4. **The trade exists only because of the override.** Under the book, max one buy, the day ended with the put. The override went CALL-only at 08:43, after the 4.8-point rip off the 738.84 low. From then on QQQ added at most 0.58 (743.62 to 744.20).

## Was the add a mistake?

Yes.

- It was outside every rule in play. The 2-buy cap was used. The bounce rule needs a green HA close, and the 09:10 to 09:12 HA candles were red (09:10 and 09:12 were green on real candles).
- The tape had turned against it. The 09:06 bar was the first close under EMA20 since 08:32 (743.21 against 743.35), a PUT bar on the book's side rule. By 09:10 EMA20 was rising 0.03 a bar, flat into the price, the MACD line was down to 0.36, and the histogram was still negative (−0.083). Holding a flat EMA20 inside a box is the coil, not a bounce.
- It was the same trade at twice the size. At about 7 cents a point, 0.12 fits QQQ near the 09:11 low (743.44), about 0.3 under the first fill, inside the same box. Its stop sat near 742.7, about 0.15 under the first lot's. Both stops filled within two minutes (09:18:37 and 09:20). The add turned −$7 into −$13.
- The cap had already done its job. The ticket logs later bounce signals as blocked by `max_buys`. On one reading of the touch rule (see "Not verified") the first of them was 09:08, three bars before the add.

## Would the flip skip or one-bar confirmation have changed it?

Not the entry. Both would have refused the add, if the bot had been the one placing it.

- Flip skip (five bars): it passes 09:00. The last put signal was the 08:32 flush bar, 28 bars earlier. It refuses any call from 09:07 to 09:11, because the 09:06 PUT bar is in the window. That covers the 09:08 bounce and the 09:11 add.
- One-bar confirmation: it passes 09:00. The 09:01 bar confirmed (green HA, close 744.045 over EMA20 743.17 and VWAP, RSI14 68.0). It would have bought the 09:01 close, 0.30 higher and 0.155 under the day's high, and stopped the same way. It refuses the add: neither the 09:10 nor the 09:11 bar was a CALL signal, so there was nothing to confirm.
- The other fixes in the 732P note don't refuse the entry either. The RSI extension block (no call over 65) passes it by 0.1 on the ticket's 64.9 and refuses it by 0.07 on the recompute, which is a coin toss, not a fix. The VWAP distance passes (1.78). The capitulation mirror never fires; no bar near the highs was a doji.
- Both fixes are built for a first signal on the wrong side, after a flip or a failed cross. This trade had neither. The side had held for 28 bars, and the trade failed by stalling.

## Pointers

1. Only buy a bounce off a real dip. If price went flat and the EMA rose to meet it, that's a stall.
2. Check the +20% price against the day's high. If it's above, the trade needs a breakout. Wait for the break or skip it.
3. Don't add to a losing 0DTE call. The second buy only doubled the loss on the same idea.
