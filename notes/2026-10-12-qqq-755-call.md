## What was wrong

The cross was legal and low quality.

1. **It was a reversal into overhead supply.** QQQ opened at 749.64, 1.63 under Friday's 751.27 close, and flushed from the 749.79 first-bar high to 747.10 at 08:34, with RSI14 about 17–20. The call came on the fifth green HA bar of the bounce: 2.33 off the low, 0.21 under the open print, 0.36 under the first-bar high, and under the 749.60–750.39 premarket range from 08:15 to 08:29. The 08:39 high of 749.45 was the top. Nothing after the fill traded above the 749.43 entry close through 08:54. Volume faded into the cross (73–89k on the last three bounce bars, 113–185k on the flush), and the first bar after the fill was red on 126k.
2. **It was thin and late against the EMA.** The HA close was 0.06 through EMA20. The real close was 0.37 through. EMA20 was still falling, down 0.15 over the prior five bars. An EMA cross comes at the end of a V, and this fill was the end of this one. The MACD line was −0.38 with the histogram just turned positive (+0.04). On the 10-02 note's numbers, that is the mirror of the 08:55 put probe.
3. **It depended on the seed.** The bot's EMA20 includes premarket bars and read 749.065 at 08:39. Seeded on regular hours only, EMA20 was 749.456, and no HA close or real close got above it from 08:30 to 08:54. The morning's only call cross exists on one seed.
4. **It failed on the first bar.** The 08:40 bar closed at 748.66, 0.37 under EMA20, with the HA close 0.02 under. The mark was 0.105. Below +20% the book holds to the stop, so it rode to 0.07.

Two structural costs, not execution errors:

- **The wing needed a breakout to arm its exits.** The 755C moved about 4 cents per QQQ point (0.135 mid at 749.43, 0.105 at 748.66, 0.085 at 748.21). +20% is a 0.168 mark, roughly +0.7 to +0.8 on QQQ, about 750.1–750.2. That is through the open print and the first-bar high and into the premarket range. The stop filled with QQQ in the 08:42 bar's 748.14–748.57, about a point under the entry. At 08:40, with over six hours to the close, the ≤$0.20 rail buys a 5.57-point wing.
- **The only bullet went ten minutes after the open.** With max one buy, the first legal cross spends the session.
